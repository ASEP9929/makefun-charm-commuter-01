One line of weather for a city that does not exist — Brollyford — written to make a bored commuter smile on the way to work.

**Entry:** "Brollyford today: drizzle until 9, then sunshine, with a 100% chance the 8:15 bus arrives on time for once." (107 characters)

The forecast keeps the register of real broadcast weather — city named, weather given, percentage chance — and bends it at the last words: the one thing no commuter ever expects, the 8:15 bus arriving on time, *for once*.
